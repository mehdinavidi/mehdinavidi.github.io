Privacy Policy – ZAPDOTECH Upload for YouTube

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

ZAPDOTECH Upload is a private tool of the ZAPDOTECH channel. It uses the YouTube API Services and is used only by the channel owner for the channel's own YouTube channel. Contact: zapdotech@gmail.com

YouTube and Google

ZAPDOTECH Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • The OAuth access token and refresh token that Google issues when the channel owner signs in (scopes youtube.upload and youtube).
  • The list of our own playlists, so that a new video can be added to the right playlist.
  • Our own video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags, which we created ourselves.

We do not access data of other YouTube users, viewers or subscribers. We do not read comments, messages or contacts.

How we use it

The data is used only to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel, set their thumbnail and add them to one of our playlists. Every video is uploaded as private. Making a video public always happens manually by the channel owner in YouTube Studio.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on our own computer in a protected configuration file. They are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. Besides Google/YouTube, no other party receives any data. We store the link of each uploaded video in our own project files. No tracking, no cookies, no advertising, and nothing is stored on your device.

Deletion and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. We then delete the stored tokens immediately; stored video links are deleted on request within 7 days. For questions or deletion requests write to zapdotech@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
