ZAPDOTECH

ZAPDOTECH is a small German tech news channel with short daily videos about AI and technology.

ZAPDOTECH Upload is our private tool. It runs on our own computer and sends our own finished short videos to our own TikTok account and to our own YouTube channel as private videos. On TikTok the owner chooses for each video: send it as a draft to the TikTok inbox, or publish it directly – but only when the owner presses the publish button himself, after choosing who can see it. Nothing is ever posted automatically or on a schedule. The tool is not offered to other people.
