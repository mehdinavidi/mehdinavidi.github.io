ZAPDOTECH

ZAPDOTECH is a small German tech news channel with short daily videos about AI and technology.

ZAPDOTECH Upload is our private tool. It runs on our own computer and uploads our own finished short videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. We then review each draft in the TikTok app and publish it ourselves. The tool is not offered to other people.
