ZAPDOTECH

ZAPDOTECH is a small German tech news channel with short daily videos about AI and technology.

ZAPDOTECH Upload is our private tool. It runs on our own computer and uploads our own finished short videos to our own TikTok account as drafts and to our own YouTube channel as private videos. We then review each video and publish it ourselves in the TikTok app or in YouTube Studio. The tool is not offered to other people.
