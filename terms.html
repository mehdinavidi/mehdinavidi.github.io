1. The service

ZAPDOTECH Upload is a private tool that sends videos created by the ZAPDOTECH channel to the channel's own TikTok account – either as a draft to the TikTok inbox, or published directly when the owner presses the publish button. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

2. Use

Only the owner of the ZAPDOTECH channel uses the tool, for their own TikTok account. Every video is reviewed by the owner first. A video is only published when the owner chooses to do so himself – either in the TikTok app (draft) or with the "Publish on TikTok" button, after choosing who can see it and giving consent. Nothing is published automatically or on a schedule. All content must follow the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
