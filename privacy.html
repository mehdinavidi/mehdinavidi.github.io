Privacy Policy – ZAPDOTECH Upload

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

ZAPDOTECH Upload is a private tool of the ZAPDOTECH channel. It is used only by the channel owner for the channel's own TikTok account. Contact: zapdotech@gmail.com

What data we use

  • The TikTok access token and refresh token that TikTok issues when the account owner signs in with "Login with TikTok".
  • Basic account information (open ID and display name) to show which account is connected.
  • Our own video files, which we created ourselves, and their captions.

We do not collect data from other TikTok users, viewers or followers. We do not read comments, messages or contacts.

How we use it

The tokens are used only to upload our own videos to our own TikTok inbox as drafts (scope video.upload). Publishing always happens manually in the TikTok app.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on our own computer in a protected configuration file. They are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. No tracking, no cookies, no advertising.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). We then delete the stored tokens. For questions or deletion requests write to zapdotech@gmail.com.
